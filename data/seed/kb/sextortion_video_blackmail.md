title: Video Call Sextortion and Blackmail Scams
---
Sextortion scams commence when targets receive a video call on WhatsApp or social media from an unknown attractive profile. Upon answering, the caller displays pre-recorded intimate or compromising video footage while recording the victim's face on the screen.

Within minutes, criminals use automated software to splice the victim's face onto explicit sexual videos. The extortionist then messages the victim with the video clip, alongside screenshots of the victim's social media follower list and family contacts. They demand immediate payments of ₹10,000 to ₹50,000, threatening to broadcast the video to employers, family members, and upload it to public video platforms. In many scenarios, a second accomplice calls posing as a Delhi Police or YouTube grievance executive demanding 'takedown removal fees'.

Critical response protocol:
1. Do not transfer any money. Paying extortionists never ends the blackmail; it only confirms that you are pliable and leads to escalating demands.
2. Immediately block the numbers across all platforms, preserve screenshots of chat logs and extortion threats, and restrict all privacy settings on your social media accounts.
3. Lodge an immediate confidential complaint on cybercrime.gov.in and dial 1930; if experiencing emotional distress or anxiety, call the free Tele-MANAS support line at 14416.
