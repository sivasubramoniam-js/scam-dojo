title: Understanding UPI Collect Request and Refund Frauds
---
In a UPI collect request fraud, scammers contact users pretending to be e-commerce merchants, food delivery agents, or ride-share customer care executives. They claim that an order was accidentally double-charged or cancelled, and that an automated refund has been initiated.

The critical trick lies in how the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) functions: you NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money or credit your bank account. Entering your UPI PIN is only required when authorising an outgoing debit from your bank. Fraudsters deliberately send a 'Collect Request' (a pull transaction) through UPI payment apps and instruct the victim to tap 'Approve' and enter their 4- or 6-digit UPI PIN to 'accept the refund voucher'. The moment the PIN is submitted, funds are debited from the victim's account and transferred to the scammer.

Key rules to protect yourself:
1. Entering your secret UPI PIN always transfers money OUT of your bank account.
2. Legitimate refunds credit your original payment method automatically without requesting any OTP, PIN, QR code scan, or collect acceptance.
3. If an unfamiliar collect request arrives on your UPI app, immediately decline it and report the UPI ID on the NPCI dispute redressal portal.
