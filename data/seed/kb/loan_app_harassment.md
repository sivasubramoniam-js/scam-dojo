title: Predatory Instant Loan Apps and Extortion Harassment
---
Predatory digital lending applications trap vulnerable borrowers with promises of instant, collateral-free personal loans approved in 60 seconds with no credit score checks. These fraudulent apps are promoted via social media advertisements and distributed via sideloaded APK links or fake storefronts.

During installation, the rogue app demands extensive smartphone permissions, including access to contacts, photo gallery, location, and SMS messages. Once approved, the app disburses only a fraction of the promised amount (e.g., ₹3,000 against a ₹5,000 loan) while deducting exorbitant upfront processing charges. Within 5 to 7 days, recovery agents begin abusive harassment, demanding exorbitant repayment fees. If payment is delayed, agents access the harvested contacts and photos, threatening to circulate morphed or obscene images to friends, family members, and colleagues.

Remediation and safeguards:
1. Borrow strictly from Reserve Bank of India (RBI) registered Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and scheduled commercial banks.
2. Never install sideloaded APK files or grant access permissions to your private contact book and photo gallery for loan approvals.
3. If targeted by harassment or extortion, do not succumb to ongoing blackmailed payments; file an immediate complaint on cybercrime.gov.in and reach out to local law enforcement.
