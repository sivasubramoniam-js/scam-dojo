title: Bank KYC Expiry and Account Suspension SMS Scams
---
Fake KYC SMS scams are among the most pervasive phishing attacks in India. Victims receive alarming text messages or automated robocalls warning that their bank account, wallet, or SIM card will be deactivated by midnight due to incomplete Know Your Customer (KYC) documentation.

The message includes an urgent link pointing to lookalike phishing domains such as bank-kyc-update.example or bit.ly shorteners. When clicked, these pages replicate official mobile banking portals and prompt victims to enter sensitive credentials: full card numbers, CVV, NetBanking usernames, passwords, and the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to their phone. In other variations, callers impersonate bank compliance officers and guide victims through downloading remote desktop apps (such as AnyDesk or TeamViewer) to 'help update records', allowing attackers full remote visibility of OTP notifications.

Crucial defense rules:
1. No commercial bank or government agency will ever ask for your OTP, ATM PIN, or CVV over the phone, via email, or through third-party SMS links.
2. Official banks never mandate downloading remote screen-sharing tools to verify KYC.
3. If your account actually requires KYC updates, visit your home branch in person or navigate independently via your bank's authenticated mobile application.
