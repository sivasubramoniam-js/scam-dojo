title: Digital Arrest and Impersonation of Law Enforcement
---
Digital arrest scams represent a severe form of psychological extortion. Criminals contact citizens via phone or video calls (Skype, WhatsApp) posing as police officers, CBI agents, cyber crime investigators, or customs inspectors.

The caller claims that an illegal courier package containing drugs, forged bank cards, or weapons has been confiscated by customs with the victim's Aadhaar or mobile number attached. Scammers use realistic-looking police station backgrounds, police uniforms, fake badges, and counterfeit court arrest warrants to induce panic. Victims are told they are under 'digital house arrest' and forbidden from hanging up or speaking to relatives. To 'verify innocence' and avoid physical imprisonment, victims are coerced into liquidating their savings, fixed deposits, or mutual funds and wiring the entire amount to a 'Government Safe Reserve Escrow Account' for forensic verification.

Essential facts:
1. 'Digital arrest' does not exist in Indian criminal procedure. No statutory law permits police or judicial authorities to arrest anyone over a video call.
2. Legitimate police or intelligence agencies never conduct interrogations via Skype, nor do they demand money transfers to clear accusations.
3. If threatened with digital arrest, immediately hang up, inform your family, and report the caller's details to 1930 and Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu portal).
