title: Fraudulent Stock Trading Groups and Guaranteed Return Schemes
---
Investment and share trading fraud has proliferated via private messaging applications (WhatsApp and Telegram). Users find themselves added to exclusive VIP investment channels claiming to be managed by renowned institutional asset managers, foreign hedge funds, or SEBI-registered portfolio advisers.

Inside the group, multiple accounts (operated by confederates or bots) post screenshots of multi-lakh daily profits, thanking the 'Sensei' or 'Trading Master' for guaranteed returns of 200% to 500% weekly on IPO allotments and institutional block trades. Victims are guided to register on proprietary web dashboards or download third-party trading APKs. While fictitious trade gains appear on the simulated interface, victims find that withdrawing funds requires paying heavy 'clearing taxes', 'margin top-ups', or 'SEBI penalty fees'. Once victims stop depositing money, the organizers delete the chat group.

Critical safeguards:
1. Guaranteed returns in the stock market are illegal under Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regulations; genuine trading always carries market risk.
2. Verify all investment advisors and stock brokers on SEBI's official public register (sebi.gov.in) before committing funds.
3. Never invest via random WhatsApp/Telegram groups or wire funds into personal savings accounts for institutional trading.
