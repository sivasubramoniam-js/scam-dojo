title: Part-Time Work-From-Home and Task Rating Traps
---
Work-from-home task scams typically initiate through unsolicited WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS invitations claiming that global marketing agencies are hiring remote staff to rate Google Maps places, like YouTube videos, or review hotel listings.

The scam is engineered around psychological baiting and sunk cost. Victims are initially tasked with 2 or 3 trivial tasks, for which scammers promptly pay a small reward (₹100 to ₹300) directly into the victim's UPI account. This fabricated payoff builds artificial trust. Next, victims are shifted to private groups and introduced to 'Prepaid High-Yield Tasks' or 'Crypto Merchants' where they must deposit ₹1,000, ₹5,000, or ₹20,000 to unlock significantly higher returns. While virtual balances in the fraudulent web portal appear to grow rapidly, any attempt to withdraw triggers demands for additional 'tax clearances', 'security deposits', or 'account upgrade penalties'.

Key protection rules:
1. Legitimate employers pay you for your labor; they never demand that you pay a deposit or upfront recharge to withdraw your salary.
2. Unsolicited recruitment messages on WhatsApp or Telegram offering extraordinary daily wages for basic clicks are guaranteed scams.
3. Once a platform refuses withdrawal without an extra deposit, stop paying immediately and call 1930.
