title: International Parcel and Customs Duty Impersonation
---
Courier parcel scams begin with an automated SMS or phone call purportedly from international logistics providers (e.g., FedEx, DHL, or India Post). The automated voice announces that a parcel addressed in your name has been held at customs due to suspicious cargo or unpaid duties.

When the victim connects to the fake operator, they are informed that their parcel contains illegal contraband (such as MDMA, counterfeit passports, or undeclared currency) bound for an overseas destination. The scammers assert that unless an immediate customs clearance penalty or verification bond is wired through UPI or IMPS, an FIR will be registered. In many instances, the call is transferred seamlessly to a confederate impersonating a narcotics officer.

Protection guidance:
1. Reputable international couriers and customs authorities do not demand personal UPI transfers or immediate cash settlements over the phone.
2. Official customs duties are billed formally with official assessment documentation through authorized customs clearing channels.
3. Check the tracking ID directly on the official postal or courier website, never through links provided in SMS messages.
