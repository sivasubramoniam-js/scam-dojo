title: Electricity Bill Disconnection and Utility SMS Scams
---
Electricity bill fraud relies on immediate artificial panic. Citizens receive an SMS warning: "Dear Consumer, your electricity power connection will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM because previous month's bill was not updated. Please immediately contact our electricity officer at 98xxxxxx."

Because power outages cause immediate domestic hardship, victims act hurriedly and dial the 10-digit mobile number provided in the SMS. The scammer on the line poses as an accounts officer from the state electricity distribution board (e.g., BESCOM, TANGEDCO, Tata Power, BSES, or MSEDCL). The scammer claims that while payment was made, a technical glitch prevented backend synchronization. To prevent disconnection, the victim is instructed to transfer a nominal fee (such as ₹10 or ₹50) via a custom APK or through screen-sharing tools like AnyDesk to 'verify the consumer meter ID', capturing the victim's banking credentials during the transaction.

Safety checklist:
1. State electricity boards send official notices with your registered Consumer Number / CA Number, never from personal 10-digit mobile numbers.
2. Disconnection procedures require formal statutory written notices days in advance; power is never terminated overnight via an ad-hoc SMS.
3. Pay and verify utility bills exclusively through authorized utility websites or standard banking apps.
