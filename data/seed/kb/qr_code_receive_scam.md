title: QR Code Scams on Online Classifieds and Marketplaces
---
QR code fraud commonly targets individuals selling furniture, electronics, vehicles, or household items on online classifieds portals like OLX, Quikr, or Facebook Marketplace. 

Shortly after listing an item, the seller is contacted by an eager buyer who agrees to the full asking price without negotiation. To finalize the deal, the buyer claims they want to send an immediate token advance via UPI or an online wallet. Instead of requesting the seller's UPI ID or phone number to transfer money, the fraudster sends an image of a QR code via WhatsApp or chat, instructing the seller: "Scan this QR code with Google Pay or PhonePe to instantly receive the advance payment of ₹10,000 into your account."

The reality of QR technology:
1. Scanning a QR code is exclusively used to SEND money or make payments. You NEVER need to scan a QR code to receive money.
2. When you scan a QR code and enter your secret UPI PIN, money is deducted from your balance and credited to the scammer.
3. If an interested buyer claims they can only pay by sending you a QR code to scan, discontinue communication immediately.
