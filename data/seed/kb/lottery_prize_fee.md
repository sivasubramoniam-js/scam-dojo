title: Lottery Winnings, Lucky Draws, and Advance-Fee Fraud
---
Advance-fee lottery scams inform targets that their mobile number, email address, or name was selected as the grand winner of a multi-crore cash prize in a national lucky draw, television quiz contest (e.g. KBC lottery), or promotional draw by an automaker or tech giant.

The notification arrives through audio recordings, letters bearing forged logos of the Reserve Bank of India, or WhatsApp congratulatory certificates. To disburse the substantial winnings (e.g., ₹25,00,000), the 'Lottery Manager' informs the winner that statutory processing costs must be settled in advance: TDS tax deductions, currency conversion charges, customs certificates, or bank transfer authorization fees. Scammers continue demanding incremental payments under novel regulatory pretexts until the victim exhausts their savings.

Key realities:
1. You cannot win a lottery or prize draw that you never purchased a ticket or entered into.
2. Legitimate prize payouts legally deduct applicable TDS from the prize amount directly before disbursement; they never demand advance cash transfers.
3. Official government bodies like the Reserve Bank of India do not hold public lottery funds or issue prize certificates.
