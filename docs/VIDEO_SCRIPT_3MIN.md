## 🛠️ Recording Setup & Pro Tips

### 1. Browser & Resolution Setup
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p, 60fps) or 4K.
- **Browser Window**: Chrome at 100% scale (or 110% for crisp typography). Hide bookmarks bar (`Cmd+Shift+B`).
- **Dark Mode**: Keep the UI in dark mode for maximum contrast and high-tech cybersecurity aesthetics.

### 2. Pre-Loaded Tabs (For Seamless Zero-Lag Switching)
Have these 4 tabs open in order so you can switch with `Cmd+Option+Right`:
1. **Tab 1**: `http://localhost:3000` (Scrolled to Hero, with India Map ready).
2. **Tab 2**: `http://localhost:3000/train` (Audio/Video Call drill preloaded).
3. **Tab 3**: `http://localhost:3000/check` (Suspicious SMS or screenshot ready to click "Analyze").
4. **Tab 4**: `http://localhost:3000/recover` (Google Maps locator and 1930 triage view).

### 3. Voice & Audio
- Keep microphone input volume balanced.
- Allow the scammer’s 24kHz live PCM voice synthesis (`Fenrir`) to be audible for 2–3 seconds during the audio call scene before resuming narration!
